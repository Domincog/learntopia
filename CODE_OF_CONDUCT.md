# Code of conduct

Learntopia exists to help people learn. The community around it should feel the same way a good
lesson does: patient, clear and on the learner's side.

## Our standards

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers. Everyone was a beginner once.
- Be specific. Criticise ideas, wording and code — never people.
- Assume good faith, and ask before you assume the worst.
- Welcome people of every background, age, ability, identity and level of experience.
- Give credit, and accept correction gracefully.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults or put-downs, discriminatory language or imagery,
sharing others' private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and any event or
channel run in the project's name. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break
these standards and may, after a warning where appropriate, block people who persist.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the contact details on the
repository's profile page. Reports are kept confidential.

This text is adapted from the spirit of the Contributor Covenant (<https://www.contributor-covenant.org>).
